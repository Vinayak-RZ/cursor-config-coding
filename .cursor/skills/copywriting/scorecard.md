# Copy scorecard

Run after every draft. Core flags block shipping. Line flags mean rewrite the
line. Pair with [../readme/anti-slop.md](../readme/anti-slop.md).

## Core (any Flag = not ready)

| # | Check | Flag if | Fix |
|---|-------|---------|-----|
| 1 | One idea, one reader, one promise, one action | Several arguments, "everyone", two asks | Name the one idea. Cut the rest. |
| 2 | Awareness match | Pitch to a cold reader, or a lecture to a buyer | README: Promise + proof. Cold marketing: problem or story first. |
| 3 | Sophistication match | Plain promise in a tired market | Lead with the mechanism or a checkable contrast. |
| 4 | Three checks on the idea | Needs a decoder, unbelievable, or any rival could sign it | Simplify, shrink the claim, or find the un-copyable fact. |

## Lines (hook, promise, bullets, CTA)

| # | Test | Flag if | Fix |
|---|------|---------|-----|
| 5 | Visualize | "solution", "better way", "seamless", "experience" | Zoom until you can drop it on a foot. |
| 6 | Falsify | Adjective pile with nothing to point at | Point: command, number, file, screenshot. |
| 7 | Nobody else | A competitor could paste it under their logo | Build on the real mechanism or the real limit. |
| 8 | Two seconds | Needs a careful read | Cut to the one thing they need in that glance. |

## Structure

| # | Check | Flag if | Fix |
|---|-------|---------|-----|
| 9 | Lead fit | Cold audience gets an offer; README gets a sales letter | Pick the lead in SKILL.md. |
| 10 | Hook earns the next line | Windup, greeting, "In this README" | Delete everything before the real hook. |
| 11 | Body holds | Middle repeats the pitch | Each section is new proof or a named limit. |
| 12 | Proof, not adjectives | "powerful", "production-ready", "trusted" | Soften to what the tree can reproduce. |
| 13 | One action | Vague or stacked CTAs | Verb + the thing they get. README: the proof command. |
| 14 | Anti-slop | Fails the anti-slop **Pass** | Rewrite. Earned contrast may stay; empty upgrade goes. |

## Verdict

- Core flag → not ready
- Core pass, line flags → right idea, wrong sentences
- Core + lines pass, structure flag → close
- Mostly pass → ship; results teach

A version you only "kind of like" is a signal to keep going.
